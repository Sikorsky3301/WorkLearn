Net revenue fell 12% in Q3 against Q2, and the entire drop sits in the wholesale channel. Web and phone were flat within noise, so this is not a demand problem. The March discount programme raised wholesale order volume 8% while cutting net revenue per order by 21%, which means we bought volume at a loss. Our top segment of 340 customers, worth 41% of revenue, ordered 15% less often. The test used to justify the discount was underpowered: at 900 orders per arm it could not detect an effect below 9%, and we observed 3%. I recommend pausing the wholesale discount for Q4 and holding list price. I also recommend contacting the 340 lapsing accounts before the quarter closes, and re-running the test on one region in Q1 sized to detect a 3% effect.
